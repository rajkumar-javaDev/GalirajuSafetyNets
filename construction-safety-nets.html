Construction Building Fall Arrest Nets

Essential debris protection and worker fall arrest netting for high-rise building construction sites.

Professional Construction Safety Nets Solutions

High-rise construction sites require reliable fall arrest systems to protect site workers, pedestrians, and adjacent properties from falling construction debris. Lakshmi Enterprises supplies heavy-duty construction safety nets engineered for extreme load absorption.

Key Benefits of Choosing Lakshmi Enterprises

  • ✅ Prevents High-Altitude Worker Fall Fatalities
  • ✅ Catches Falling Debris, Bricks, Tools & Concrete Rubble
  • ✅ ISI Certified Material Tested to International Standards
  • ✅ Easy to Relocate as Floor Construction Advances
  • ✅ All-Weather UV & Moisture Resistant Construction

Material & Technical Specifications

Dual-layer netting consisting of a 4mm thick braided nylon structural safety net combined with a fine 1mm debris containment mesh overlay.

Our Step-by-Step Installation Process

Outrigger steel arms are cantilevered from building slab edges, guy ropes are anchored to concrete columns, and double-layer construction nets are rigged along the perimeter.

Ideal Applications

Commercial high-rise construction, residential tower builds, bridge infrastructure projects, and facade renovation works.

Maintenance & Care Tips

Our safety nets and anti-bird spikes require virtually zero maintenance. Periodically wash the netting surface with clean water spray to remove dust. Avoid using sharp tools or acidic chemical cleaners near the anchor points.
