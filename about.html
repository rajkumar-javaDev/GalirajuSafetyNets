About Lakshmi Enterprises

Pioneering safety netting and bird control solutions engineered for uncompromised home and commercial security.

Our Company Story

Lakshmi Enterprises was founded with a singular mission: to protect families, children, pets, and property from high-rise balcony hazards and bird infestations without altering the architectural beauty of modern homes.

Over years of dedicated service, we have successfully completed over 5,000 installations across high-rise apartments, luxury villas, commercial office towers, and industrial complexes. Our commitment to industrial-grade UV-stabilized materials and precision fitting has earned us a trusted reputation.

Our Core Mission & Vision

Mission: To deliver 100% reliable, aesthetic, and long-lasting safety netting and invisible grill solutions with zero compromise on material quality, installation precision, and customer satisfaction.

Vision: To remain the most trusted safety netting partner by pioneering UV-stabilized monofilament technology, marine-grade SS 316 invisible grills, and physical written warranties.

Quality Standards & Certified Materials

We exclusively utilize Garware High-Density Polyethylene (HDPE) translucent nets, UV-treated anti-bird spikes, and marine-grade SS 316 steel wire cables. Every safety net is tested to withstand over 150 kg of breaking tension, guaranteeing maximum impact resistance against windstorms, bird pecking, and accidental falls.
